# Research Board — role cards (R1)

**Eight analytical seats; NOT eight automatically running agents or eight licensed professionals.** Named personnel, if later assigned, must provide real authenticated participation and relevant qualifications; otherwise the roles are explicitly *simulated review perspectives*. Nobody may pretend to be a registered midwife, PRC examiner, psychometrician, or student respondent.

| Seat | Analytical persona | Core challenge | Must explicitly disagree about |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | Education Research Lead | Which learning outcomes and cognitive processes are evidence-supported for this population? Audit intervention methodology, effect, transfer, publication bias | Overclaiming spaced/retrieval effect sizes or exam-pass gains |
| **B2** | Philippine MLE Blueprint Analyst | What do verified PRC TOS, examination programs, CHED BSM and missing scans actually establish? | Invented exam weights, item-format percentages, unofficial "leaked" blueprints |
| **B3** | Situational Item & Assessment Designer | How should original vignettes, distractors, key rationales and difficulty levels be authored and reviewed? | Vignettes that reward cue spotting rather than reasoning; wrong/ambiguous keys |
| **B4** | Clinical/Professional-Scope Evidence Auditor | Does *each* fictional claim, referral/scope assertion or response match applicable authoritative source version and PH setting? | Administrative/payer forms used as clinical authority; theoretical answer promoted to independent care authority |
| **B5** | Instructional Experience Architect | How do we scaffold explanation, retrieval, worked examples, transfer and accessible feedback across novice/returning learners? | A beautiful screen without demonstrable instructional function |
| **B6** | Accessibility, Language & Learner Equity Advocate | What breaks for low-bandwidth phones, keyboard/screen readers, uncertain English, shared devices or learners with interrupted study? | Timing pressure, shame/streaks, unaudited translations, visual-only evidence |
| **B7** | Assessment Science & Method Skeptic | Are constructs, comparisons, proposed measurements and inferences valid? How could each proposal fail? | Fake psychometrics, mastery percentages, self-selection, data leakage, untested scheduling |
| **B8** | Editorial Integrity & Forge Convener | Establish actual role/authority, evidence register, recorded debate, unresolved dissent, exact SHA and owner decision requests | False consensus, fabricated participants, unverified citations, premature implementation |

### Seat controls

- **B4 is not a qualified clinical reviewer by being named B4.** Real medical licensure/clinical-review attestations require a verifiable person, consent and a bounded review. Documented source verification remains the current project publication direction, not a new mandatory recruitment gate.
- **B2 never asserts exact current PRC item patterns unless the official materials demonstrate them.** Practice formats are explicitly *Komadrona-designed*.
- **B7 has a challenge mandate:** every accepted-sounding proposal needs an alternative, a falsifiable test and a possible disconfirming result.
- **B8 records true author/session identity.** When one AI session answers all eight seats, label its work `SINGLE_SESSION_MULTI_LENS`, not an independent swarm.
- Seat discussions are organized into **written deliberative arguments**, not private chain-of-thought dumps. The board publishes rationale, evidence, alternative, disagreement, and actual test reports.
- **Human authority:** KIRCH final decisions. Nox has a separate independent source/contract review lane (GitHub Issue #4) and is not silently drafted as a B-seat or treated as approval.
- Distinguish feedback from actual examinees (only if gathered) from opinions about what students might need.

### Minimum cross-examination assignments

- B1 ↔ B7: retrieval/spacing evidence vs confounds.
- B2 ↔ B3: official exam fidelity vs proposed original situational practice.
- B4 ↔ B3: scenario/answer validity and professional scope.
- B5 ↔ B6: interactive learning ambitions vs accessible low-bandwidth alternatives.
- B8 ↔ every seat: source/version/permission and dissent custody.

**Activation format (when a real session exists):** `BOARD-R1 / B3 / actual actor [identity] / HYP-004 / evidence + objection + proposed falsifier`. Simulated lenses must say `SIMULATED B3 ANALYSIS by [actual actor]`, and must not create fake participant receipts.
