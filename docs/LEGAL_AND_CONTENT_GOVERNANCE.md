# Legal, Privacy, and Content Governance

This document is an internal project control. It is not legal or medical advice.

## Public identity

Komadrona Review must always be presented as an independent educational project. It must not claim or imply affiliation, approval, sponsorship, review, partnership, or endorsement by PRC, the Professional Regulatory Board of Midwifery, DOH, CHED, a school, or a review center unless documented authorization is obtained and the wording is separately approved.

Agency and institutional names may be used accurately to identify laws, official issuances, and sources. Official seals, logos, and visual identities are excluded unless written permission clearly covers the proposed use.

## Educational limitation

Public pages must state that the project:

- supports independent study only;
- is not an official reviewer;
- is not a clinical protocol or patient-specific advice;
- does not replace formal instruction or supervised clinical training;
- does not guarantee examination eligibility, coverage, accuracy in every circumstance, or a passing result; and
- requires users to verify current official examination and clinical guidance.

## Examination-content rule

The repository must not contain:

- actual or claimed actual licensure-examination questions;
- leaked, recalled, reconstructed, predicted, or confidential questions;
- commercial reviewer scans or proprietary test banks;
- faculty examinations supplied without clear rights; or
- instructions for obtaining restricted examination content.

Practice questions must be original and supported by traceable sources.

## Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order where applicable:

1. Current PRC and Board of Midwifery programs and resolutions
2. Philippine statutes and official issuances
3. Current Department of Health guidance and national programs
4. Current CHED standards and competencies
5. Current WHO and other recognized primary international guidance
6. Peer-reviewed literature and properly licensed educational references

AI output, social-media posts, user-upload repositories, and unsourced review notes are not final authorities.

## Content record

Every review topic should retain:

- domain and topic identifier;
- source title and issuing body;
- publication date or version;
- relevant page, section, or paragraph;
- date checked;
- author or editor;
- review status;
- correction history; and
- next review date when guidance is likely to change.

## Review statuses

- `Draft` — incomplete or not cleared for reliance
- `Source checked` — core statements compared against identified sources
- `Clinically reviewed` — reviewed by a qualified professional with documented scope and consent
- `Needs re-verification` — possible change or expired review interval
- `Archived` — removed from current study paths but retained for history

No person may be credited as a clinical reviewer without actual review and consent.

## Privacy boundary

Version 1 avoids a centralized learner database. The website does not request names, email addresses, schools, student numbers, phone numbers, health information, quiz screenshots, or proof of completion.

Browser progress is local and may be deleted by browser or device changes. Public copy must avoid claiming that the project processes no data at all because hosting providers, external quiz services, and voluntary communications may independently process technical or identifying information.

No analytics, advertising trackers, social widgets, or user-submitted forms may be added without updating the privacy notice and this governance document first.

## Google Forms boundary

Each form must be inspected before publication. It should not collect names or email addresses, should not require file uploads, and should permit repeated practice. The site must disclose that Google Forms is external and governed by Google's practices.

## Copyright and licensing

- Repository source code: MIT License
- Original articles, original practice questions, and original illustrations: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless otherwise stated
- Third-party materials: original rights and licenses remain in effect

Public availability is not treated as permission to copy. Link to official materials where practical. Use only necessary, limited quotations with attribution. Do not reproduce complete books, tables, diagrams, or question banks without clear permission or license.

## Corrections and complaints

A public correction route must be available before the content library launches. Reports involving personal data, examination leaks, or patient information must not be posted publicly. Valid corrections should be logged with the affected content, source, change, date, and reviewer.

## Funding boundary

Version 1 does not include donation QR codes, payment links, paid access, advertisements, or commercial sponsorship. A later funding feature requires a separate review of Philippine solicitation rules, hosting-plan restrictions, privacy disclosures, accounting, wallet ownership, and public reporting before code is added.
