# Komadrona Review — Content Reference Register

Version: 1.0  
Verified: 3 August 2026

This register identifies the baseline sources approved for drafting Version 1 content. The application-readable source of truth is `src/data/referenceRegistry.ts`.

## Control rule

A source being listed here does not automatically authorize publication of a claim. Every article and question must still identify the exact section, page, recommendation, or statutory provision used. Philippine law, PRC issuances, CHED standards, current DOH policy, professional scope, referral systems, and facility protocols take priority for Philippine-specific questions.

WHO publications are evidence-informed supporting guidance and must be checked against current Philippine policy before being converted into instructions.
