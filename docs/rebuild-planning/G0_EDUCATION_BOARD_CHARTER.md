## The decision we are designing, not rushing

A midwifery board-review website must be designed as a coherent educational system. We need an explicit evidence basis for *how* it teaches, what its question forms assess, how situations are constructed, what answer feedback achieves, when it revisits material, how it communicates uncertainty and which performance claims are safe. These are not solved by an attractive website, PRC headings, or a large generated MCQ set.

**Research outcome:** propose an owner-ready learning and examination-experience blueprint with independently inspectable evidence; produce paper-only lesson and situational-case examples; preserve disagreements, adverse cases and unresolved questions. Do **not** implement or publish content in this mission.

## Board shape

Eight disciplined analytical seats: B1 research; B2 PRC blueprint; B3 situational assessment; B4 clinical/PH-source auditor; B5 instructional experience; B6 accessibility/equity; B7 measurement skeptic; B8 integrity/convener. The seats are *roles*, not new authenticated agents. Antigravity may route to separately started workers, but no worker exists or acts merely because this document lists them. When one AI session assumes all seats, label `SINGLE_SESSION_MULTI_LENS`.

**Minimum true independence:** distinct actual people/sessions independently producing evidence and critique at the same frozen source SHA, with genuine author identity shown. B4 label never implies clinical licensure. Independent reviews must not edit their own adjudicated candidates.

## Round protocol

| Round | Question | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **R0** Source/authority verification | What is actually approved/official vs inferred? Which papers read? | Source ledger; status and limits |
| **R1** Competing hypotheses | Which teaching and board-practice alternatives plausibly work, for which learner? | Named HYP cards, comparators, dissent |
| **R2** Skeptical cross-examination | Where do studies not transfer, exam claims overreach, questions fail, or learners suffer? | Adversarial challenge log and revised proposals |
| **R3** Paper experiments | What designs pass exact-content, learner-journey, question-ambiguity and accessibility reviews? | One end-to-end nonclinical exemplar + situational item *spec* + actual paper-test evidence |
| **R4** Owner-facing synthesis | What design principles are evidence-backed, disputed, optional or rejected? | Dissent-preserving decision memo; owner deliberation |

No "18 hours nonstop" or fabricated test loops. Each actual round is bounded by evidence and decisions. Further research stops when decision impact is low, an affordable test is more informative, or the question must be deferred.

## One honest and useful first board question

**Which approach best prepares a novice for unfamiliar original board-style situations without falsely claiming clinical competence?**

Comparator A: primarily direct MCQs after concise lessons.  
Comparator B: novice worked examples → short recall → guided fictional scenario → independent original item → later review.  
Comparator C: question-first adaptive practice with source-backed explanation on failure.

No winner is declared. Compare cognitive-load risk, item validity, actual verified source burden, time and accessibility; challenge transfer from medicine/nursing studies to Philippine midwifery. Run a *paper* walkthrough before claiming learning effects. Record uncertainties.
