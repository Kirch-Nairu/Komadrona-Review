### Evidence considerations

- Retrieval/spacing: Trumble 2023/2024, Martinengo 2024; credible support for the *general principle*, not these precise variants.
- Worked examples/cognitive load: van Merriënboer & Sweller 2015; not an RCT of this website.
- Case/simulation: nursing/medical education studies are indirect context for Philippine MLE; do not conflate perceived self-efficacy and clinical competence.
- Board fidelity: only dated official PRC publications establish official facts, not paper-internal guesses.
- HYP-009/010: derived as safety/logical invariants from project contract, not clinical-effect studies.
- Hypotheses are deliberately open for revision after board cross-examination. No `PASS` is recorded.

### Protocol to promote a hypothesis

Each hypothesis needs an exact cited evidence card, a counterargument, an experimental or paper-test method, observed data, and a written decision with limits. For human/learner experiments, identify sampling, recruitment, consent/privacy, equal exposure, matched difficulty, transfer target, pre-registration and data interpretation before running; these studies are **not required to approve every research concept**, but no claimed impact is justified without suitable observation.
