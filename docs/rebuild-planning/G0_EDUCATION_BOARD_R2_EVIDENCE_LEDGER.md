## 3. Summary of Evidence Governance Principles

1. **Exact-Source Verification:** No clinical assertion, drug dose, or procedural step may be published without an exact document, edition, page number, and paragraph locator from a Tier 1 or Tier 2 authority.
2. **Rejection of Administrative Authority for Clinical Steps:** Billing circulars (PhilHealth) and administrative health center forms (DOH Form v1.2024) must never be used to substantiate midwife procedural competence.
3. **Preservation of Known Blueprint Gaps:** The 14 missing items in Fundamentals Midwifery Procedures and `A.1–A.11` in Primary Health Care must remain documented as unverified gaps. KOMADRONA will not invent missing official competencies.
4. **Separation of Educational Intent from Regulatory Quotas:** Situational questions are authored because they foster clinical reasoning, not because an unverified regulatory quota requires them.
