## Relations and maturity

- **M-01/M-11** anchor what may legitimately be claimed about clinical/examination content.
- **M-04/M-05/M-06** anchor the learning experience; `Explain → Recall → Reason → Revisit → Reflect` is a proposed cycle, not a guaranteed optimal method.
- **M-02/M-07/M-08/M-09** anchor situational-item assessment validity; a realistic vignette can still be a bad question.
- **M-03/M-10/M-12** anchor board simulation conditions, learner feedback, and honest results.
- No single matrix can certify "licensure readiness", professional safety, or publication on its own.
- A matrix moves to `RECOMMENDATION_READY` only when evidence, alternative, risk and falsifier are documented; it becomes `OWNER_ACCEPTED` only on an actual KIRCH decision.

## Priority sequence — research, not implementation

**Stage 1:** M-01, M-02, M-04, M-07, M-08, M-11 (official fidelity and clinical/assessment safeguards).  
**Stage 2:** M-05, M-06, M-09, M-10 (how to teach, test, remediate and retain).  
**Stage 3:** M-03, M-12 (mock experience, equitable presentation, scoring and privacy) — investigate early enough to avoid retrofits.  
**Then:** cross-matrix integrated critique of one complete paper-only learning and board-practice journey.
